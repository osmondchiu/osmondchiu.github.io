<h1>About</h1>

<p>
Osmond Chiu is a public policy professional focused on rebuilding public sector 
capacity and strengthening democratic institutions.
</p>

<p>
He is a Senior Policy and Research Officer at the Community and Public Sector Union 
and a Research Fellow at the Per Capita think tank. His work centres on strengthening 
public institutions, improving workforce capability, and advancing evidence-based 
policy reform in Australia.
</p>

<p><b>Key focus areas:</b></p>
<ul>
  <li>Public sector reform, capability, and workforce sustainability</li>
  <li>Outsourcing, privatisation, and service delivery models</li>
  <li>The future of work and labour market policy</li>
  <li>Multiculturalism, diversity, and political representation</li>
  <li>Social democracy and institutional reform</li>
</ul>

<p>
Osmond has over a decade of experience in public policy roles across the Australian 
Public Service and the trade union movement. His work spans policy development, 
research, and advocacy on the future of work, public sector capability, inequality, 
and democratic representation.
</p>

<p>
He is a regular contributor to public debate, writing on Australian politics, social 
democracy, and multiculturalism. His work has appeared in publications including the 
Sydney Morning Herald, The Guardian, Canberra Times, South China Morning Post, 
Jacobin and Tribune.
</p>

<p>
His commentary and analysis have also been featured in domestic and international 
media, including the New York Times, BBC and CNN.
</p>

<p>
Osmond was recognised as one of the 40 most influential Asian Australians under 40 in 2021 and was a participant in the 2026 Chinese Australian Community Leaders Summit.
</p>
