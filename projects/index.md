## Publications and Writing

My publications focus on public sector reform, workforce policy, social democracy, and multicultural representation. My work spans academic research, policy analysis, and commentary across leading Australian and international outlets.

---

## Key Focus Areas

- **Public sector reform and workforce policy** — including APS capability, outsourcing, and employment conditions  
- **Multiculturalism and political representation** — including multicultural participation, institutional diversity, and democratic inclusion  
- **Labour movement and political economy** — including inequality, union policy, and social democratic reform  
- **Australia–China relations and diaspora studies** — including Sinophobia, foreign interference debates, and diaspora engagement
