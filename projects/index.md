<p>My work spans academic research, policy analysis, and commentary across major Australian and international outlets.</p>

<h2>Key Focus Areas</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Public sector reform and workforce policy</b> — including APS capability, outsourcing, and employment conditions.</li>
  <li><b>Multiculturalism and political representation</b> — with a focus on CALD participation, diversity in institutions, and democratic inclusion.</li>
  <li><b>Labour movement and political economy</b> — covering inequality, union policy, and social democratic reform.</li>
  <li><b>Australia–China relations and diaspora studies</b> — including Sinophobia, foreign interference debates, and diaspora engagement.</li>
</ul>
