## Policy Work

I focus on strengthening our public institutions through evidence-based policy reform.

My work spans public sector reform, workforce policy, and economic policy, with a particular interest in institutional capability, inequality, and the role of government in economic change. I also work on multicultural representation and public discourse, including about the Chinese diaspora in Australia.

I draw on my experience across the public service, trade union movement, and think tank sector, combining policy analysis, research, and advocacy.

---

## Key Areas of Work

### Public Sector Reform
- Size, structure, and capability of the Australian Public Service  
- Outsourcing, privatisation, and service delivery models  
- Governance, accountability, and institutional performance  

### Workforce Policy
- Employment conditions and job security  
- Workforce planning and capability development  
- The future of work, including automation and digital transformation

### Social and Economic Policy
- Inequality, wages, and living standards  
- Community wealth building and progressive economic policy  
- Public policy responses to economic change  

### Multiculturalism and Representation
- Diversity in political and public institutions  
- Chinese diaspora in Australia  
- Representation of multicultural communities  
- Race, identity, and public discourse

## Approach

My approach combines quantitative analysis, comparative policy research, and direct engagement with stakeholders.

I draw on official datasets (including ABS and OECD data), policy modelling, and consultation with workers, institutions, and communities to develop practical, evidence-based policy recommendations.
